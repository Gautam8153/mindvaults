What Mind Vault Actually Contains

Mind Vault lists 9 named ingredients on its label. Here is what each one is included for:

  • Vitamin B12 — A well-known B vitamin commonly included in cognitive formulas to support normal nerve function and healthy energy metabolism, which many people associate with clearer thinking.
  • Vitamin B6 — Frequently paired with B12 in memory and focus blends to support normal neurotransmitter activity and the body's everyday cognitive signaling.
  • Folate (Vitamin B9) — A B vitamin often grouped with B6 and B12 in brain-health blends to support healthy homocysteine levels and normal cell function.
  • Lion's Mane Mushroom — A functional mushroom widely used in nootropic-style supplements and commonly linked with memory support and mental clarity in traditional and modern cognitive formulas.
  • Bacopa Monnieri — An Ayurvedic botanical that appears often in memory-focused capsule products and is traditionally used for supporting recall and relaxed concentration.
  • Ginkgo Biloba — One of the most recognized botanicals in cognitive wellness formulas, traditionally used to support healthy blood flow and everyday mental sharpness.
  • Phosphatidylserine — A phospholipid found in cell membranes and a staple ingredient in memory and focus supplements, typically included to support normal cognitive function.
  • Alpha-GPC — A choline compound common in nootropic blends, included to support acetylcholine-related pathways that are tied to attention and mental clarity.
