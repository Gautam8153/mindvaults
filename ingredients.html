What Are The Ingredients In Mind Vault?

Mind Vault is formulated with 9 named ingredients — including Vitamin B12, Vitamin B6, Folate (Vitamin B9), Lion's Mane Mushroom, Bacopa Monnieri — each listed individually below with the role it plays in the formula, so you can see exactly what you are taking before you buy.

1

Vitamin B12

A well-known B vitamin commonly included in cognitive formulas to support normal nerve function and healthy energy metabolism, which many people associate with clearer thinking.

2

Vitamin B6

Frequently paired with B12 in memory and focus blends to support normal neurotransmitter activity and the body's everyday cognitive signaling.

3

Folate (Vitamin B9)

A B vitamin often grouped with B6 and B12 in brain-health blends to support healthy homocysteine levels and normal cell function.

4

Lion's Mane Mushroom

A functional mushroom widely used in nootropic-style supplements and commonly linked with memory support and mental clarity in traditional and modern cognitive formulas.

5

Bacopa Monnieri

An Ayurvedic botanical that appears often in memory-focused capsule products and is traditionally used for supporting recall and relaxed concentration.

6

Ginkgo Biloba

One of the most recognized botanicals in cognitive wellness formulas, traditionally used to support healthy blood flow and everyday mental sharpness.

7

Phosphatidylserine

A phospholipid found in cell membranes and a staple ingredient in memory and focus supplements, typically included to support normal cognitive function.

8

Alpha-GPC

A choline compound common in nootropic blends, included to support acetylcholine-related pathways that are tied to attention and mental clarity.

9

L-Theanine

An amino acid naturally found in tea, often added to cognitive formulas to promote a calm-but-alert feeling without a stimulant edge.
